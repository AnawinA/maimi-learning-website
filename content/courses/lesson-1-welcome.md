## What is MaiMi?

**MaiMi** is a gamified learning platform that lives entirely in your browser. There's no account to create, no server to log into. Your progress is saved right here, in your browser's local storage.

> "The secret of getting ahead is getting started." — Mark Twain

## How it Works

Every lesson is a simple Markdown file. You read through it, scroll to the end, and click **Finish Lesson** to mark it complete.

Here's what happens when you finish a lesson:

1. ✅ The lesson is saved as **completed** in your browser
2. 🏆 You earn **XP** (experience points)
3. 🗺️ The **course map** updates — your node turns **gold**
4. 🎉 A **celebration** pops up to cheer you on

## What to Expect

Each course is made up of several lessons like this one. They're designed to be read top-to-bottom, like an article.

You'll see a **progress bar** at the very top of every lesson — it fills as you scroll down. Once you hit the bottom, the **Finish Lesson** button unlocks.

---

## Your Data is Yours

All progress is saved with `localStorage`. You can:

- **Export** it as a JSON file to back it up
- **Import** it on another browser or device
- **Clear** it to start fresh

Head to the **Settings** page anytime to manage this.
